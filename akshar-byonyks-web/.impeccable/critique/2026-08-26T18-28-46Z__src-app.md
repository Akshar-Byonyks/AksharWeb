## Design Specificity Verdict

Category-interchangeable below the fold, genuinely authored above it.

Shape inventory: the 1px `#d8e2e8` rounded rectangle appears **12x on Home, 23x on the X-1 page**. One border colour, three radii. Below each hero every section repeats the same composition: left H2, lead paragraph, row of those rectangles — five consecutive times on each page.

This is DESIGN.md working as written. Flat-By-Default, 1px borders not elevation, no shadows, no gradient text, no kickers, accents rationed to chips — every rule is a prohibition. The system specifies what not to do and almost nothing about what a page's visual substance is. Applied to a page with two images, restraint produces vacancy rather than calm.

Deterministic scan: `detect.mjs` over `src/app` + `src/components` = 1 finding (`text-[0.8rem]` in `ui/button.tsx:36`, pre-existing shadcn `sm` variant, unused). Clean because the rules are being followed perfectly.

## What's Working

- **Pending-data language.** `Figure pending source citation` in dashed amber converts missing sourced statistics into visible institutional honesty. Specific to a company whose principle is "evidence before claims."
- **Register discipline.** Patient plain-language and clinician precision sit sections apart and sound like one company.
- **X-1 hero composition.** Device right, argument left, ink ground, honest render caption. Proves the system can do asymmetry.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Hero India stats physically dissolve

The three "dialysis gap in India" cards sit on the ink-to-white gradient. Bottom borders disappear entirely; the lower half floats on lightening ground with white text. The emotional core of the India argument looks like a rendering error on the first screen.

**Fix:** end the gradient above the stat band, or move the band onto solid ink.
**Command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P0] Ink is the brand and Home spends 9% on it

Highest-leverage change on the site. DESIGN.md's Full-Bleed Rule confines ink to two moments to protect rarity — the result is a 69%-white page whose signature colour is a garnish. The rule also forces every below-hero section onto white, which is *why* five identical white card-rows happen.

**Fix:** raise ink to ~35-40% of page height, assigning ground by meaning (the wayfinding logic already claimed for accents, extended to grounds): INK = home/night/patient life; WHITE = evidence/regulation/specification.
**Command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### [P1] Only product asset used at a quarter of its potential

X-1 render (genuine alpha channel, no backing plate needed) shown at ~250px on Home, ~260px on the X-1 page. Should be 600-900px, once per page, bleeding past the container on ink. Natural extension: an annotated device callout with leader lines to the four features — converts four identical rectangles into one authored diagram in the same grammar as the spec table.

**Command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Trust payload looks like a footnote

510(k) / ISO 13485 / 10,000+ is the densest credibility on the site (Priority-1 investors, Priority-3 clinicians). Renders as three plain text blocks on white, 242px tall, zero visual treatment, directly beneath the dissolving gradient.

**Fix:** real band — ink ground, oversized numerals, existing accent roles.
**Command:** `/impeccable bolder`

### [P2] Legal and contact pages use 43% of the viewport

Privacy: 622px measure centred in 1440px = 818px empty white on each side across 2753 continuous px. The measure is correct; nothing else is there. Contact has the same shape at 47%.

**Fix:** sticky section index in the left column — genuinely useful on a nine-section legal document.
**Command:** `/impeccable layout`

## The Ownable Idea Being Missed

This company exists because of a geography problem: dialysis in India means travelling to a clinic three times a week; the X-1 moves it to a bedroom overnight. The accent system encodes it (gold = home/India), the logo encodes it (globe, India in gold), the access-geometry scene encodes it. Then the idea stops and never recurs.

1. **India as a recurring graphic field** — not a data map (no sourced statistics exist, must not invent), but India as geometry: section ground, future `/innovation/market/` hero, watermark in the closing ink mass. Needs zero new facts.
2. **The night.** "The cycler runs on its own while the household sleeps" is the most human sentence on the site, set at 14px as a caption. Ink is already the colour of night — spend the P0 ink budget here first.
3. **Distance as a repeated motif.** Three trips a week vs one night at home — drawable as pure geometry with no claims attached.
